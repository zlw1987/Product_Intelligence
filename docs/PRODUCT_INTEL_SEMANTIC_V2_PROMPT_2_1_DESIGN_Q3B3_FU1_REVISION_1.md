## 2. The three-axis decomposition (C1, C9)

### 2.1 Why revision 0's matrix was wrong

Revision 0's P1–P6 matrix was a **packaging-state** matrix: one dimension (the sales-unit evidence state) crossed with loosely worded trigger conditions that embedded the identifier decision inside cell P3 ("the identifier relation is established per Section 7"). Two defects followed:

1. **The three questions were conflated.** "Is the candidate the same physical product?", "Is the candidate sold in the same commercial unit?", and "May this candidate price automatically?" are different questions with different owners. Revision 0 answered them in one table; the observed 2.0 failure mode (the model treating the tier-policy requirement line as a decision blocker) is exactly the conflation of question 3 into question 1, and the P3 cell repeated a milder version of it (the sales-unit state silently inheriting the identifier answer).
2. **The identifier side of P3 was under-defined.** "Established" meant different things in different cells (exact MPN vs consistent prefix vs SKU-not-target vs attribute match), and the alignment test ("aligned or uncontradicted") made uncontradicted absences count as evidence — which is how missing identity evidence became systematically non-critical (C6) and how generic overlap became sufficient for MATCH (C4).

### 2.2 The axes

**Axis 1 — Physical-product identity (IA).** Question: *Does the supplied evidence ground that the candidate is the same physical product as the target requested?* States (defined in §5.2–§5.3):

| IA state | Meaning | Frozen shapes it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IA-EXACT | Identifier relationship established by an exact published identifier: the requested MPN published with identity wording (MPN field or title token, no compatibility/reference/SEO context), or a published SKU the deterministic layer records as identical to the target | U1 (identity wording), U2/SKU_EQUALS_TARGET |
| IA-AUTH | Identifier relationship established by explicit `MANUFACTURER_RELATION_AUTHORITY` covering a complete-different near-miss difference | U5 with labeled authority (0020) |
| IA-PARTIAL | A consistent partial form (U3 prefix) **plus** distinctive alignment of all request-conveyed hard dimensions (G2) | U3 (0010) |
| IA-DISTINCT | No decisive identifier (U4 no-MPN, U2/SKU_NOT_TARGET) **plus** distinctive alignment of all request-conveyed hard dimensions (G3) | U4 (0014, 0017), U2N (0008, 0035, 0039) |
| IA-GENERIC | Product overlap is generic: at least one request-conveyed hard identity dimension is unpinned (not published, or published strictly coarser) on the candidate side; no published conflict | U3 thin (0012), U4 thin (0015, 0043), U2N thin (0009) |
| IA-UNRESOLVED-DIFF | A complete different published part number (U5) whose difference is not authority-resolved and whose meaning is unproven on the supplied evidence | U5 not established (0018, 0021, 0022, 0023) |
| IA-CONFLICT | A published identity conflict (hard, or reviewable not authority-resolved) or a published accessory/role difference | all NO_MATCH conflict cases (0002–0004, 0007, 0011, 0016, 0019, 0024–0031, 0036, 0040, 0042) |
| IA-CONTRADICTED | The supplied evidence contradicts itself on a request-conveyed dimension, leaving it unpinned in both directions | 0037 |

**Axis 2 — Commercial sales unit (SU).** Question: *What does the supplied evidence establish about the candidate's commercial sales unit relative to the target's commercial unit?* States:

| SU state | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| SU-EQ | Proven equal: published packaging evidence (structured OBSERVED channel or clear raw cue) establishes the same commercial unit as the target's |
| SU-X | Published incompatible: published packaging evidence (structured OBSERVED or clear raw cue) incompatible with the target's commercial unit — **the absolute rule** (approved decision 6) |
| SU-AMB | Ambiguous cue: a raw cue is present but its packaging meaning/contents are unproven (e.g., "kit" without contents) |
| SU-UNK | Unproven, not claimed: channel UNAVAILABLE and no cue anywhere — equivalence **unproven and not asserted in either direction** |
| SU-ENT | Unproven and entangled (decision-critical): SU-UNK **and** the identifier difference itself may carry the sales-unit/packaging/revision question (U5 not established shape — the Micron R/T shape) |

**Axis 3 — Downstream pricing eligibility (TIER).** Question: *May this candidate's evidence enter pricing output?* Owner: **frozen S2-A, byte-untouched.** The derivation is `tier = S2-A(decision, confidence, product-evidence-quality (A), structured conflicts, relationship requirement (B), near-miss flags, V2_AUTHORITY_QUALIFIED=False)`. It is rendered in §4 **as an explicit output column of the matrix, never as a model input**, and §2.3 states its independence.

### 2.3 Independence of the tier derivation (C9)

1. **Direction of information flow is one-way:** input → (model, 2.1 rules) → decision + structured conflicts + confidence → (frozen S2-A) → tier → (gates + DRAFT policy) → pricing eligibility. The 2.1 prompt tells the model the requirement line is on this downstream path and is *not* its input (approved decision 3); it never tells the model what tier any outcome reaches, and the tier derivation never sees the prompt's internal reasoning.
2. **The tier derivation does not see the sales-unit state.** Its inputs (decision, confidence, dimension-A quality, conflict classes, requirement, near-miss flags) contain no sales-unit channel. A MATCH under SU-UNK therefore derives the *same* tier as a MATCH under SU-EQ — the commercial-safety consequence of an unproven unit is bounded entirely by (i) the advisory nature of the semantic layer (an LLM is never the sole authority for exact identity), (ii) the frozen matrix rows (only MATCH+HIGH+STRONG can reach `AI_ASSISTED_COMPARABLE`, before ceilings), (iii) the live evidence-quality ceiling (live profiles are at most LIMITED → at most NEEDS_REVIEW, §11.4), (iv) `NEEDS_REVIEW` being **never pricing-eligible** (frozen import-time invariant), and (v) `V2_AUTHORITY_QUALIFIED = False` (persist-only).
3. **No new path to pricing authority.** The tiers reachable from a 2.1 MATCH are a subset of the tiers reachable from a 2.0 MATCH — the same 27-entry matrix, the same ceilings, the same `PRICING_ELIGIBLE_TIERS`. Machine Price (`MACHINE_VERIFIED`) remains reachable only through deterministic exact identity, which 2.1 does not touch. 2.1 changes the *probability* that the model emits a MATCH (recall), not the *set of authority outcomes* any emission can reach.
4. **What revision 0 got right is preserved:** the four-way separation table (§7.1 of revision 0) becomes the three decision-relevant axes above plus the explicit frozen tier column; the 2.0 failure was the model importing axis-3 policy into axis-1 decisions, and the "READING THE DETERMINISTIC IDENTITY CONTEXT" clause remains the correction.

## 3. The precise meaning of MATCH with `sales_unit=UNAVAILABLE` (C2)

**The frozen output schema has no sales-unit assertion field.** The six response keys can express a sales-unit position only through the three structured attribute lists and the conflict classes:

| Expressible position | Encoding | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| proven equal unit | PACKAGING_QUANTITY in `matched_attributes` (with detail) | the published evidence establishes the same commercial unit |
| proven different unit | PACKAGING_QUANTITY / BUNDLE in `conflicting_attributes` + class in `conflict_classes` | the absolute rule: NO_MATCH (row N-1) |
| unproven and decision-critical | PACKAGING_QUANTITY / BUNDLE in `missing_critical_attributes` | UNCERTAIN (row U-2, SU-ENT) |
| **not determined** | **PACKAGING_QUANTITY / BUNDLE absent from all three lists** | **the contract-defined fourth state: the sales-unit question was not decided on the supplied evidence, in either direction** |

**An advisory MATCH under SU-UNK asserts exactly two things, and asserts nothing about unit equality:**

1. **Physical-product identity is grounded** on the supplied evidence per §5 — at the resolution of the strongest identifier ground available (part-number resolution under IA-EXACT/IA-AUTH; **description resolution** under IA-PARTIAL/IA-DISTINCT, i.e., "the same product as the request describes, on everything the request describes").
2. **No published evidence shows a different commercial unit:** the candidate's packaging is not established to differ from the target's commercial unit (no SU-X observation, no clear raw cue).

It does **not** assert: single-unit status, equal pack quantity, tray/retail equivalence, or per-unit price comparability. `UNAVAILABLE` is a recorded absence ("the listing published no packaging / sales-unit field — do not infer a single unit", frozen 2.0 text preserved), and the absence of PACKAGING_QUANTITY/BUNDLE from all three lists in a MATCH response means "not determined", which neither the model, the harness, downstream code, nor a human reader may read as "proven single unit" or "proven equal unit".

**Why this is not "treating UNAVAILABLE as proof of single-unit equivalence":** single-unit equivalence would be a *positive claim* (the candidate is sold as one unit, therefore price-comparable). The MATCH under SU-UNK makes *no claim about the unit at all*: it is a claim about the product plus a recorded absence about the packaging. The asymmetry that the frozen prompt protects ("a false MATCH is materially worse than UNCERTAIN") is preserved where the evidence actually supports a claim — the identity claim — and deliberately not extended to a claim the evidence does not carry. The safety does not rest on the model abstaining from an unprovable unit question (that was the 2.0 over-abstention: 11/11 universal PACKAGING_QUANTITY abstentions), it rests on the frozen downstream path: the tier derivation cannot see the unit state, live tiers cap at NEEDS_REVIEW, NEEDS_REVIEW never prices, and V2 is unqualified (persist-only). A false *identity* MATCH under SU-UNK is bounded by the same gates and precision floor as any MATCH; the *unit* question remains open for the human-review overlay, which is the only confirmation path in the frozen authority contract.

**Downstream effect, stated once:** a SU-UNK MATCH enters `SEMANTIC_OUTCOME_TIER_MATRIX` exactly like any MATCH (row by confidence × dimension-A quality), then through the same ceilings. ## 5. Corrected per-substate decision semantics (C3, C4, C5)

The deterministic layer has already established the identifier shape (state, substate, signals, normalized keys) — the model does not re-litigate the shape; it applies the rule for the recorded substate. "Request-conveyed hard identity dimension" means a hard identity dimension (PRODUCT_FAMILY, GENERATION, CAPACITY, INTERFACE, FORM_FACTOR, PRODUCT_ROLE) whose value the target's MPN description publishes. "Pinned" means the candidate side publishes a value for that dimension that matches the conveyed value under normalized equivalence ("3.84 Terabytes" = "3.84TB"; "U.3 2.5in" pins U.3 with consistent additional detail). "Strictly coarser" means the candidate publishes a value that does not contradict but does not pin the conveyed value ("DDR5" vs conveyed "DDR5-6400").

### 5.1 U1_TITLE_MPN

The exact requested MPN token in the title is **identity wording** unless the surrounding wording is compatibility / reference / SEO ("compatible with", "replacement for", "drop-in", "upgrade from", "fast upgrade from X"): then it is not an identifier ground (the published product role decides — an accessory is N-2). With identity wording: IA-EXACT (M-1). A bare token (nothing else published, 0005) is identity wording (no marker present); attribute gaps are **not** decision-critical with G1 (§6.1 part 3) — MATCH is the contract default, UNCERTAIN remains defensible (label CONTESTABLE; watch R13). A published conflict on any identity dimension still decides N-2 regardless of the exact identifier (0003 CAPACITY, 0004 GENERATION, 0031 ACCESSORY).

### 5.2 U2_SKU_ONLY / U3_PARTIAL_BOUNDARY / U4_NO_MPN (the corrected identifier grounds)

**U2 / SKU_EQUALS_TARGET:** the published SKU is frozen-2A identical to the target (the deterministic comparator is the authority): IA-EXACT (M-1). The identifier ground never overrides a published product-role conflict (0007: SKU equal, product is an adapter cable → N-2).

**U2 / SKU_NOT_TARGET (C5):** a different retailer SKU is **not a conflict** — it never grounds NO_MATCH, never enters the conflict set, and is never read as a different product (retailer SKUs are not manufacturer identifiers; frozen 2.0 rule preserved). It is also **not an identifier ground** — the absence of a conflict is not proof of equivalence. The identifier axis is undecided by the SKU; identity is decided **entirely** by the product evidence under the DISTINCTIVE-ALIGNMENT TEST (§5.2 below, same as U4): pinned → IA-DISTINCT (M-4); generic → IA-GENERIC (U-1). The rendered requirement line (REVIEWED_RELATION_AUTHORITY_REQUIRED for this shape) is downstream tier policy (§5.4): it caps the derived tier, it does not gate the decision, and it is never read as "unresolved identifier relation".

**U3 / PARTIAL_BOUNDARY (C3):** the candidate's published MPN is a **consistent truncation/prefix** of the requested MPN (frozen shape; the prefix property is established, not re-litigated). The corrected semantics, in full:

- A consistent prefix is **not a conflicting full part number**: the candidate did not publish a different complete number; the prefix cannot ground NO_MATCH on its own, and the U5 entanglement (SU-ENT) does **not** apply — the gap is an unpublished tail of the *requested* identifier, not a candidate-side variant claim.
- A consistent prefix is **not automatically proof of exact product identity**: the unpublished tail may encode a different variant (capacity, packaging, revision).
- **Sufficient positive evidence** — the prefix becomes an identifier ground (IA-PARTIAL, M-3) when, and only when, all three hold, **each testable on the supplied evidence alone** (no catalog context is required or permitted):
  - **(U3-a)** the frozen shape is U3_PARTIAL_BOUNDARY (consistent prefix; established fact);
  - **(U3-b)** **every request-conveyed hard identity dimension is pinned** on the candidate side (matching published value, normalized equivalence, credit-all-first over structured facts *and* raw title/specification text);
  - **(U3-c)** **no supplied evidence indicates a different variant**: no raw packaging/revision/bundle cue, no published contradiction, no unresolved published reviewable difference, no internally contradictory request-conveyed dimension.
- Where (U3-b) fails, the unpublished tail *may carry* the difference for exactly the unpinned dimensions: they are decision-critical → **U-1** (UNCERTAIN naming them; 0012: prefix + title "NVMe data center SSD" vs requested "1.92TB U.2" → CAPACITY, FORM_FACTOR unpinned → UNCERTAIN naming [CAPACITY, FORM_FACTOR] — the label's recorded expectation). Where a published contradiction exists → **N-2** (0011: prefix + "8TB" vs requested 4TB → NO_MATCH CAPACITY; the prefix is irrelevant to a published conflict).
- **Anchor (0010):** requested `MTFDKCC3T8TGP-1BK1DABYY` / "Micron 7500 4TB U.3 NVMe SSD"; candidate publishes MPN `MTFDKCC3T8TGP` + brand Micron + "Micron 7500 4TB U.3 NVMe SSD". (U3-a) frozen shape ✔; (U3-b) every request-conveyed hard dimension pinned: PRODUCT_FAMILY (7500/SSD) ✔, CAPACITY (4TB) ✔, FORM_FACTOR (U.3) ✔, INTERFACE (NVMe) ✔; (U3-c) no variant cue, no contradiction ✔ → IA-PARTIAL → **MATCH**. The label's REVIEWED_SOURCE_GROUNDED citation (the prefix is the recorded 4D-D catalog family prefix) is **not transmittable** through any frozen input channel (RC-3) and is **not relied upon**: the derivation above uses only the payload. Recorded as risk R3, not as a dependency.

**U4 / NO_MPN (C4):** no usable candidate identifier; the identifier question does not exist (frozen requirement NOT_APPLICABLE — there is nothing to establish; preserved 2.0 rule: a missing candidate MPN is not automatically NO_MATCH). Identity is decided **entirely** by the product evidence under the DISTINCTIVE-ALIGNMENT TEST:

- **DISTINCTIVE (IA-DISTINCT, M-4):** every request-conveyed hard identity dimension is pinned on the candidate side, no supplied evidence indicates a different product or variant, and no published identity conflict (hard or reviewable) exists. Anchors: 0014 ("96GB DDR5-6400 MRDIMM Server Memory Module" pins 96GB / DDR5-6400 / MRDIMM / server memory — the request's full conveyed spec set) → MATCH; 0017 ("3.84TB U.3 NVMe SSD, data center" pins 3.84TB / U.3 / NVMe / data-center family; the only published difference is CONDITION Refurbished vs new — price-only, may be reported, never blocks) → MATCH.
- **GENERIC OVERLAP (IA-GENERIC, U-1):** the candidate publishes only a subset of the request-conveyed hard dimensions, or a strictly coarser value for one of them — a different product or variant remains consistent with everything published. Anchors: 0015 (title "NVMe SSD" vs requested "3.84TB U.3 NVMe data center SSD" — CAPACITY and FORM_FACTOR unpinned) → UNCERTAIN naming [CAPACITY, FORM_FACTOR] (the label's recorded expectation); 0043 ("3.84TB NVMe SSD" — FORM_FACTOR unpinned) → UNCERTAIN default, MATCH defensible (label CONTESTABLE); 0009 (U2N, "96GB DDR5 server memory" vs requested "96GB DDR5-6400" — speed strictly coarser, INTERFACE unpinned) → UNCERTAIN default, MATCH defensible.
- **Published conflict (N-2/N-3):** 0024–0030 (capacity/generation/interface/form-factor contradictions), 0036 (multi-conflict), 0016 (cross-category PRODUCT_ROLE), 0042 (ACCESSORY_RELATION), 0040 (published different BRAND — reviewable: never MATCH; NO_MATCH default, UNCERTAIN inspection pole).
- **Internal contradiction (U-5):** 0037 (title 3.84TB vs specification text 7.68TB — CAPACITY unpinned in both directions → UNCERTAIN naming CAPACITY; never NO_MATCH on the contradiction alone; approved decision 7).

### 5.3 U5_NEAR_MISS_MPN (NM-1 truncation / NM-2 substitution)

The candidate publishes a **COMPLETE** part number different from the requested one in a bounded near-miss shape (frozen: NEAR_MISS_TRUNCATION / NEAR_MISS_SUBSTITUTION). Corrected semantics:

1. **Never automatically equivalent** (frozen 2.0 rule preserved). A complete different number is itself published identifier evidence: it **blocks the DISTINCTIVE-ALIGNMENT TEST** — aligned product attributes cannot override the candidate's published claim to be a specific different number (anchors 0018/0022: full attribute alignment, yet not MATCH).
2. **Equivalence is established ONLY** by `MANUFACTURER_RELATION_AUTHORITY` explicitly establishing the relationship (or manufacturer-stated evidence resolving the difference as not material). A customer-retrieval relation and a manufacturer product context **never** establish it (approved decisions 4, 5). Established → IA-AUTH (M-2, reason MATCH_AUTHORIZED_IDENTIFIER_RELATION; the resolved difference is not reported as a conflicting revision). Anchor 0020.
3. **Not established:** the difference is either *resolved-different* (the supplied evidence shows it corresponds to an actual product difference — then N-3 where the number itself is the judged conflict, or N-2 where published hard conflicts decide as in 0019: CAPACITY + INTERFACE published-conflicting alongside the number shape) or *unproven-different* (the difference's meaning — revision, packaging, sales unit, variant — is not established on the supplied evidence). Asserting `MPN_IDENTITY` requires the resolved-different reading; the unproven-different reading is UNCERTAIN, never a manufactured conflict.
4. **SU-ENT (row U-2):** where the unproven difference *may carry the sales-unit/packaging/revision question* — the unpublished suffix could denote a packaging or sales-unit variant and no manufacturer-stated evidence resolves it — the missing sales unit is decision-critical: **UNCERTAIN naming PACKAGING_QUANTITY** (and/or BUNDLE). Anchors 0018/0022 (the R/T suffix; customer-retrieval-only or no relationship; the sales-unit-safety flag: a MATCH here is the CRITICAL false MATCH the case exists to catch). The customer-retrieval relation makes the variant question concrete (the alias says the pair is related for retrieval) while conferring **zero** equivalence authority — it is never described to the model as manufacturer equivalence, and it never licenses MATCH.
5. **Revision-shaped (row U-3):** 0021/0023 (01/02 substitution, identical titles, no sales-unit-safety flag, no authority / product-context-only): the difference may carry a REVISION question (reviewable, resolvable by inspection) → default **UNCERTAIN**; the NO_MATCH pole is permitted where the model judges the published difference to establish a different product (label CONTESTABLE {NO_MATCH, UNCERTAIN}); MATCH is excluded on both poles (row precedence: not IA-AUTH, distinctive alignment blocked by rule 1).

### 5.4 The "Relationship requirement" line (approved decision 3, preserved from revision 0)

The rendered line is **downstream authority-tier policy**: it records whether the candidate's derived pricing-authority tier requires a reviewed relationship source to be ESTABLISHED. It is **not** the model's decision input. It neither blocks nor permits a MATCH: a MATCH on the supplied evidence is possible where the requirement is REVIEWED_RELATION_AUTHORITY_REQUIRED (the requirement then caps the derived tier — which the model cannot see), and a NO_MATCH or UNCERTAIN is required where the evidence does not support equivalence even though the requirement is NOT_REQUIRED/NOT_APPLICABLE. This is a faithful restatement of the frozen S2-A semantics (`substate_relationship_requirement`: the tier "is capped at NEEDS_REVIEW ... when — and only when — the requirement is REVIEWED_RELATION_AUTHORITY_REQUIRED and dimension B is not ESTABLISHED"; S2-A amendment 1: the AI may still return MATCH/HIGH; it simply cannot auto-price). The two-line user-template annotation (§8.3) is unchanged from revision 0.

### 5.5 The DISTINCTIVE-ALIGNMENT TEST (single definition, used by U2N / U3 / U4)

The product evidence **distinctly aligns** the candidate with the requested product when:

1. **Pinning:** every hard identity dimension the target request conveys through its MPN description is pinned on the candidate side — a published matching value (normalized equivalence), credited from structured facts **and** raw observation text (credit-all-first: "credit ALL supplied evidence on both sides before judging; a value published on the candidate and uncontradicted is NOT missing").
2. **No variant indication:** no supplied evidence (either side, structured or raw) indicates a different product or variant — no published contradiction, no raw packaging/revision/bundle cue, no unresolved published reviewable difference, no internally contradictory request-conveyed dimension.
3. **Bounded by supplied evidence:** the test is judged only on what is published on the two sides. The model does not use catalog knowledge or outside product knowledge to imagine unpublished variants ("Do not rely on unstated specifications, catalog knowledge, or outside product knowledge" — frozen 2.0, preserved).

A dimension the request does **not** convey is never decision-critical for identity and is never named missing (fixes the 0035 BRAND/PRODUCT_FAMILY mis-crediting direction and the 0017 BRAND/GENERATION/REVISION_OR_SUFFIX, 0039 FORM_FACTOR over-demands). A value published by the candidate but not conveyed by the request is credited, not required (0039 MRDIMM; 0035 "PCIe Gen4"; 0014 brand).

## 6. Corrected missing-critical and contradiction semantics (C6)

### 6.1 The revised three-part definition (replaces revision 0 §7.4)

A dimension D belongs in `missing_critical_attributes` **only if all three hold**:

1. **ELIGIBLE.** D is a **request-conveyed** hard identity dimension (PRODUCT_FAMILY, GENERATION, CAPACITY, INTERFACE, FORM_FACTOR, PRODUCT_ROLE, ACCESSORY_RELATION), or D is PACKAGING_QUANTITY / BUNDLE under the SU-ENT condition (row U-2). Consequences: a dimension the request does not convey is **never** decision-critical and never named (0017 BRAND/GENERATION/REVISION_OR_SUFFIX; 0039 FORM_FACTOR); BRAND, REVISION_OR_SUFFIX, and CONDITION are **never** named missing (a published difference on them is a conflicting attribute or an authority-resolved relation; an unpublished one is resolvable by inspection — the frozen tier path for reviewable matters).
2. **UNPINNED.** The candidate side publishes **no pinning value** for D: no matching value and no strictly-coarser value (the coarser token pins nothing — 0009 "DDR5" vs "DDR5-6400"). A value published on the candidate and uncontradicted is **credited, not missing** (credit-all-first; fixes the 0035 PRODUCT_FAMILY under-credit). A published **contradiction** against the target is a **conflict** (N-2/N-3), not a missing attribute. A **self-contradictory** published pair (0037) leaves D **unpinned in both directions** (approved decision 7) — neither side may be adopted, so D is unpinned, not contradicted-against-the-target.
3. **DECISION-CRITICAL.** The identity decision **depends** on D: the supplied evidence grounds identity (IA-EXACT / IA-AUTH / IA-PARTIAL / IA-DISTINCT) **only with** D pinned. Operationally: with an exact identifier (IA-EXACT/IA-AUTH) attribute gaps are **not** decision-critical — the exact part number pins the product and an unpublished attribute is resolvable by inspection (0005 stays MATCH-default without naming anything); without one, distinctive alignment **fails on** the unpinned dimension(s) — they are precisely the gaps (0012, 0015, 0043, 0009); under SU-ENT the packaging gap is decision-critical by definition (0018, 0022).

**What this corrects (the systematic defect):** revision 0's part 2 counted "published on neither side" and "uncontradicted" as non-missing, and part 3 was conjunctive over "identifier not established **and** not all target-published dimensions aligned **or uncontradicted**" — so a dimension absent from the candidate while the published dimensions were merely uncontradicted was classified non-critical by the wording itself, and missing identity evidence (the 0015/0012/0043 shapes) was systematically under-named, while (in the opposite direction) the model's 2.0 behavior over-named unpublished-by-both-sides dimensions (0017). The revised parts replace "uncontradicted" with "pinned by a published value" and define criticality against the *grounding* (§5), not against a conjunction of weak conditions.

**And the credit-all-first rule** (preserved from revision 0): "Credit ALL supplied evidence on both sides (structured facts and raw observation text) BEFORE naming anything missing."

### 6.2 Consequences for the universal 2.0 pattern

The 11/11 universal `PACKAGING_QUANTITY` abstention (all inputs SU-UNK, none SU-ENT) is removed: SU-UNK with a grounded identity is row M-*, where PACKAGING_QUANTITY/BUNDLE are **not eligible** (part 1) and **not unpinned-as-decision-critical** (§3: the absence means "not determined"). The only SU-UNK cases that name PACKAGING_QUANTITY are the SU-ENT ones (0018/0022) — the label-pinned set.

### 6.3 Internal contradiction (approved decision 7, axis-clarified)

If the supplied evidence contradicts **itself** on a dimension (title vs specification text; one published field vs another), that dimension is **unpinned in both directions**: it is neither established nor contradicted *against the target*. No published side may be adopted as the product's value; the corresponding conflict class is never asserted; NO_MATCH is never returned on the contradiction alone. If the dimension is request-conveyed and the contradiction leaves it unpinned, it is decision-critical (part 3, via grounding failure) → **U-5**: UNCERTAIN naming the dimension. Anchor 0037: CAPACITY (title 3.84TB vs spec 7.68TB) → UNCERTAIN naming CAPACITY; the 2.0 capture's NO_MATCH/CAPACITY was the false-NO_MATCH the rule removes (label already expects UNCERTAIN — no label change needed or permitted). A self-contradictory **packaging** pair (title "single unit" vs spec "tray of 20") is the packaging-dimension instance of the same rule: the cue is unresolvable → U-4-style UNCERTAIN, never NO_MATCH on the contradiction alone (the absolute rule N-1 requires a *published*, i.e., externally consistent, incompatibility).

### 8.2 SYSTEM PROMPT 2.1 — full corrected draft text (revision 1)

Markers: **[2.0 unchanged]** = byte-identical to the frozen 2.0 section; **[2.1 NEW]** = new in the 2.1 draft; **[2.1 CHANGED]** = 2.0 text retained with stated modifications. **[R1]** = modified by this revision (Q3-B3-FU1) relative to the revision-0 draft.

```text
You are a product commercial-equivalence evaluation assistant.

TASK   [2.0 unchanged]
Your task is to determine the COMMERCIAL SEMANTIC EQUIVALENCE of a TARGET product and a CANDIDATE listing: does the candidate listing represent the same commercially comparable product as the target, and with what confidence?

You are given a structured input with five sections: TARGET, CANDIDATE LISTING, DETERMINISTIC IDENTITY CONTEXT, CONTEXT PROVENANCE, and PRODUCT EVIDENCE. Use ONLY the evidence supplied in those sections. Do not rely on unstated specifications, catalog knowledge, or outside product knowledge. Every value in the input is labeled with its evidence class (STRUCTURED FACT with a source label, RAW OBSERVATION TEXT, COMMERCIAL / PACKAGING EVIDENCE, REVIEWED TARGET CONTEXT, or AUTHORITY-SIDE PRODUCT EVIDENCE); read every value in its class.

WHAT YOU ARE NOT ASKED TO DO   [2.0 unchanged]
- Do not estimate, infer, or compare prices. Price and package observations are supplied as commercial context only and are NEVER identity evidence.
- Do not decide or override deterministic identity status. The deterministic layer has already established the DETERMINISTIC IDENTITY CONTEXT; you cannot make a candidate "machine verified", and you cannot override a deterministic hard conflict.
- Do not infer manufacturer authority. Do not turn customer retrieval relations, aliases, or compatibility/SEO wording into manufacturer equivalence or identity authority.
- Do not decide final market aggregation or any pricing outcome.

WHAT YOU SHOULD DETERMINE   [2.1 CHANGED (R1): the two questions are made explicit]
- First, whether the candidate is the SAME PHYSICAL PRODUCT as the target on the supplied evidence (the IDENTIFIER RELATIONSHIP RULES and the DISTINCTIVE-ALIGNMENT TEST). Second, separately, whether any PUBLISHED evidence shows the candidate sold in a DIFFERENT COMMERCIAL SALES UNIT (the COMMERCIAL SALES-UNIT RULES). The two questions have different answers and different outputs; answer both before deciding.
- Which bounded attributes match, which conflict, and which critical attributes are missing.
- Whether material conflicts exist, classified using the structured conflict classes below (never free text).
- Whether uncertainty remains, and what would resolve it.

EVIDENCE CLASSES (how to read the input)   [2.0 unchanged, except one [2.1 NEW] sentence in the COMMERCIAL class, preserved from revision 0]
- STRUCTURED FACT: a bounded value with an explicit source label (published structured field, listing title, specification text, or reviewed manufacturer product context). A structured fact is an OBSERVATION about one side of the comparison: it may support your reasoning, but it is not by itself a proof that the two sides agree. Equivalence between the sides is your semantic judgment.
- RAW OBSERVATION TEXT: free text as published (the listing title, the requested description). Raw text may support semantic reasoning, but a token occurring in raw text is NOT a structured fact, and raw text NEVER becomes manufacturer authority. Absence of a structured fact does not erase the raw observation evidence; the absence of both is not a value.
- COMMERCIAL / PACKAGING EVIDENCE: price, currency, availability, seller, condition, offer, and the sales-unit / packaging channel. Commercial evidence is NEVER identity evidence. The sales-unit channel is EXPLICIT: "UNAVAILABLE" means the listing published no packaging / sales-unit field. Absence of packaging evidence is NOT proof of equal sales unit and is never read as "single unit"; do not infer a packaging value from price or from any other commercial fact. [2.1 NEW] A packaging cue published in raw observation text (listing title or specification text) — for example "pack of N", "N x", "tray", "factory pack", "multi-pack", "bundle" — is ALSO packaging evidence: read it under the COMMERCIAL SALES-UNIT RULES exactly as if it were published in the structured channel.
- REVIEWED TARGET CONTEXT: when present, a structured/reviewed manufacturer context about the TARGET (never about the candidate). It may strengthen your understanding of the requested product. Its identifier relation, when present, is customer-retrieval-only: zero identity authority, never manufacturer equivalence.
- AUTHORITY-SIDE PRODUCT EVIDENCE: the PRODUCT EVIDENCE section (deterministic/reviewed only). You may observe it; you must NOT create, upgrade, or assert new product evidence: your matched_attributes are conclusions about equivalence, not new authority-side facts.
- NEVER MANUFACTURE A MISSING FACT: if a critical attribute is absent from ALL supplied evidence, name it in missing_critical_attributes. Do not fill a gap with a guess or with outside knowledge.

IDENTIFIER RELATIONSHIP RULES   [2.1 NEW (R1 revised); replaces and extends "IDENTIFIER EVIDENCE RULES"]
- The DETERMINISTIC IDENTITY CONTEXT has already established the identifier shape (state, substate, signals, normalized keys). Do not re-litigate the shape; apply the rule for the substate it records.
- An identifier relationship is ESTABLISHED only by: (i) the exact requested MPN published with identity wording (MPN field, or the exact token in the title without compatibility / reference / SEO wording); (ii) a published SKU that the deterministic layer records as identical to the target; or (iii) a complete different part number in a bounded near-miss shape, where the CONTEXT PROVENANCE explicitly lists MANUFACTURER_RELATION_AUTHORITY establishing that relationship. Nothing else establishes it: a different retailer SKU, a consistent partial form of the requested identifier, a customer-retrieval relation, and a manufacturer product context never establish it by themselves.
- An established identifier relationship is strong evidence but is not the only evidence of commercial equivalence: a published conflict on an identity dimension (hard or reviewable) still decides the outcome — a candidate identified as the requested part that is published to be a cable, a caddy, a tray, or a different capacity is a NO_MATCH.
- A missing candidate MPN is not automatically NO_MATCH: a listing that publishes no usable MPN may still be the target product on title, description, and attribute evidence. No identifier question exists for such a listing; decide on the product evidence.
- A target MPN appearing in the listing title may be product identity OR compatibility / reference / SEO wording (for example "compatible with", "replacement for", "drop-in", "upgrade from"). Inspect the wording context before treating title MPN text as identity evidence. Compatibility / reference / SEO wording does not establish the identifier relationship; the published product role then decides (an accessory is a NO_MATCH).
- U2, a different retailer SKU (SKU_NOT_TARGET): a different SKU is NOT a conflict and NOT proof of a different product (retailer SKUs are not manufacturer identifiers). It is also NOT an identifier ground: the absence of a conflict is not proof of equivalence. The decision rests entirely on the product evidence under the DISTINCTIVE-ALIGNMENT TEST.
- U3, a consistent partial form (PARTIAL_BOUNDARY): a candidate MPN that is a consistent truncation / prefix of the requested MPN is a PARTIAL FORM of the requested identifier: it is NOT a different published part number and NOT a conflicting revision, but it is NOT automatically proof of exact product identity — the unpublished tail may encode a different variant. The partial form becomes an identifier ground when, and only when, the DISTINCTIVE-ALIGNMENT TEST is satisfied: every hard identity dimension the target request conveys through its MPN description is published on the candidate side with a matching value (normalized equivalence: "3.84 Terabytes" = "3.84TB"), and no supplied evidence indicates a different variant. Where the test fails, the unpublished tail may carry the difference: the unpinned dimensions are decision-critical (UNCERTAIN, naming them) — never a NO_MATCH on the prefix alone.
- U4, no usable candidate identifier (NO_RELATION): no identifier question exists. The decision rests entirely on the product evidence under the DISTINCTIVE-ALIGNMENT TEST.
- U5, a complete different part number (NEAR_MISS_MPN: truncation of the requested form, or exactly one character substituted): a complete different published part number is NOT automatically equivalent, and it is itself published identifier evidence: it BLOCKS the DISTINCTIVE-ALIGNMENT TEST — aligned product attributes cannot override the candidate's published claim to be a specific different number. Equivalence is established ONLY by MANUFACTURER_RELATION_AUTHORITY explicitly establishing the relationship (or manufacturer-stated evidence resolving the difference as not material); a customer-retrieval relation and a manufacturer product context never establish it. When the relationship is not established: the decision is NO_MATCH only where the supplied evidence shows the difference resolves to an actual product difference (assert the corresponding conflict classes), or UNCERTAIN where the difference's meaning (revision, packaging, sales unit, variant) is unproven on the supplied evidence — do not assert MPN_IDENTITY on an unproven difference. Where the unproven difference may itself carry the sales-unit / packaging / revision question, the COMMERCIAL SALES-UNIT RULES (MISSING SALES UNIT, CRITICAL) apply and the safe definite output is UNCERTAIN. When MANUFACTURER_RELATION_AUTHORITY establishes the relationship, do not report the resolved difference as a conflicting revision.

DISTINCTIVE-ALIGNMENT TEST   [2.1 NEW (R1)]
- The product evidence DISTINCTLY ALIGNS the candidate with the requested product when: (1) every hard identity dimension (PRODUCT_FAMILY, GENERATION, CAPACITY, INTERFACE, FORM_FACTOR, PRODUCT_ROLE) that the target request conveys through its MPN description is PINNED on the candidate side — the candidate publishes a value matching the conveyed value under normalized equivalence, credited from structured facts AND raw observation text (credit ALL supplied evidence on both sides before judging); and (2) no supplied evidence indicates a different product or variant — no published contradiction, no raw packaging / revision / bundle cue, no unresolved published reviewable difference, no internally contradictory request-conveyed dimension.
- Alignment is NOT distinctive (GENERIC OVERLAP) when at least one request-conveyed hard identity dimension is not pinned: the candidate publishes no value for it, or publishes a value strictly coarser than the conveyed one (for example "DDR5" where the request conveys "DDR5-6400": the coarser token does not contradict, but it does not pin the speed). Generic overlap leaves a different product or variant consistent with everything published; the unpinned dimensions are then decision-critical.
- A value published on the candidate side that is not conveyed by the request and not contradicted is credited (it may strengthen the match) but is not required. A published contradiction on any request-conveyed hard dimension is a conflict and decides the outcome regardless of alignment elsewhere. A dimension the request does not convey is not decision-critical for identity (never name it missing).
- The test is bounded by the SUPPLIED EVIDENCE: do not use catalog knowledge or outside product knowledge to imagine unpublished variants; judge only on what is published on the two sides.

READING THE DETERMINISTIC IDENTITY CONTEXT   [2.1 NEW (preserved from revision 0)]
- The identifier facts in this section are established by the frozen deterministic layer; do not re-litigate them.
- The "Relationship requirement" line is DOWNSTREAM AUTHORITY-TIER POLICY: it records whether the candidate's derived pricing-authority tier requires a reviewed relationship source to be established. It is NOT your decision input. It neither blocks nor permits a MATCH: a MATCH on the supplied evidence is possible even where the requirement is REVIEWED_RELATION_AUTHORITY_REQUIRED (the requirement then caps a derived tier you cannot see), and a NO_MATCH or UNCERTAIN is required where the evidence does not support equivalence even though the requirement is NOT_REQUIRED or NOT_APPLICABLE. Decide solely per the IDENTIFIER RELATIONSHIP RULES, the COMMERCIAL SALES-UNIT RULES, the MISSING-CRITICAL ATTRIBUTES RULES, and the DECISION RULES.

CONTEXT PROVENANCE RULES   [2.1 CHANGED (preserved from revision 0: all 2.0 text retained; the first two bullets each gain one appended sentence)]
- CUSTOMER_RETRIEVAL_RELATION, when present, is a RETRIEVAL HINT ONLY: it explains how the candidate was found. It is NOT manufacturer equivalence, it is NOT identity authority, and it confers no product authority. Use it as a retrieval hint only. Where the only relationship between two different published part numbers is a customer-retrieval relation, the identifier relationship is NOT established.
- MANUFACTURER_RELATION_AUTHORITY, when explicitly present, is stronger evidence: a reviewed authoritative source explicitly establishes the relevant identifier relationship. Use it as labeled manufacturer relationship authority, not as a guess. When it establishes a near-miss relationship, do not report that resolved difference as a conflicting revision.
- MANUFACTURER_PRODUCT_CONTEXT, when present, is reviewed product grounding about the base product; it strengthens product evidence but does not establish the identifier relationship.

PRODUCT EVIDENCE RULES   [2.0 unchanged]
- The PRODUCT EVIDENCE section is authority-side evidence produced by deterministic or reviewed sources. You may observe it and report semantic conclusions, but you must NOT create, upgrade, or assert new product evidence: your matched_attributes are conclusions about equivalence, not new authority-side facts.

COMMERCIAL SALES-UNIT RULES   [2.1 CHANGED (R1 revised); replaces "PHYSICAL PRODUCT vs COMMERCIAL SALES UNIT"]
- Distinguish PHYSICAL-PRODUCT equivalence from COMMERCIAL SALES-UNIT / PACKAGING equivalence. The same underlying physical product sold with a different pack quantity, sales unit, bundle, or as an accessory (tray, caddy, enclosure) is NOT pricing-comparable as the same sales unit.
- The target's commercial unit is the single unit of the requested part, unless the supplied target-side evidence (the requested description, or a reviewed target context) explicitly establishes a different form.
- WHAT A MATCH ASSERTS, PRECISELY: a MATCH asserts (1) that the candidate is the same physical product as the target, grounded on the supplied evidence per the IDENTIFIER RELATIONSHIP RULES and the DISTINCTIVE-ALIGNMENT TEST, and (2) that NO PUBLISHED evidence shows the candidate sold in a different commercial sales unit. When the sales-unit channel is UNAVAILABLE and no packaging cue is published, a MATCH does NOT assert that the sales units are equal and does NOT assert a single unit: the sales-unit question is UNPROVEN and is not claimed in either direction. In the structured output, the ABSENCE of PACKAGING_QUANTITY and BUNDLE from all three attribute lists (matched, conflicting, missing-critical) means "not determined on the supplied evidence" — it must never be read, by you or any downstream consumer, as "proven single unit" or "proven equal unit".
- ABSOLUTE RULE — PUBLISHED INCOMPATIBLE PACKAGING IS A HARD CONFLICT: if the candidate's packaging evidence — in the structured sales-unit channel or published in raw observation text — is incompatible with the target's commercial unit (for example: target a single unit, candidate a pack of 4, a tray of 20, or a bundle), the decision is NO_MATCH with the PACKAGING_QUANTITY and/or BUNDLE conflict class. ## 11. Recommendation (preserved from revision 0 §12; P1 scope corrected)
```

**Prompt 2.1 alone is sufficient to resolve the observed zero-MATCH recall on the qualification corpus, and it is the necessary first step; structured packaging extraction is a complementary live-safety hardening that must not gate it, and extraction-first alone is insufficient.**

1. **Prompt-only is sufficient for the corpus (and the live decision shape).** All 11 abstentions are prompt-contract defects (the undefined criticality trigger; the unexplained requirement line; the missing U3/U4/U2N criteria; the over-demanding missing-critical definition; the missing contradiction rule), not input deficiencies: every one of the 11 is decidable on its supplied evidence under the corrected criteria (§7, 11/11 SUPPORTABLE). The live input shape is the same (§10.5). If a qualified model under 2.1 still abstains, that is model-side (T7), not contract-side.
2. **Extraction-first is insufficient.** The 11 listing pages publish no packaging field at all (by construction); a perfect extractor still leaves `sales_unit = UNAVAILABLE` on all 11 — the zero-recall persists under reading R1.
3. **Extraction (P2) is needed for live safety, independently.** It makes SU-X deterministic-evidence-grounded live and shrinks R1/R2 (the silent-multi-pack and missed-cue exposures). It changes no prompt, no label, no authority rule; the 2.1 prompt already handles OBSERVED inputs correctly (the corpus negatives prove it).
4. **Grounding (P3) is a separate track.** The authority-side matched-fact infrastructure determines whether live 2.1 MATCHes can ever reach `AI_ASSISTED_COMPARABLE`; it is orthogonal to the prompt contract and to packaging extraction.
5. **Correction (R1):** confidence guidance is **out of P1 scope** (CL-9). It was not needed for any of the eleven-case derivations, it is not part of the approved-decision preservation list, and its removal is costless to recall (confidence is not scored). If a later capture shows confidence-boundary tier problems, that is a separate bounded amendment with its own approval question.

Phasing (each phase bounded, reviewed, separately approvable): **P1** — Prompt 2.1 contract (this design) + requalification captures (primary and fallback, independent) + T1–T5, T7 evaluation. Exit criteria: eight gates pass on both models; recall ≥ 0.90 and precision ≥ 0.99 on the DRAFT policy's arithmetic; zero false MATCH; 0018/0022 remain UNCERTAIN; decision POLICY_PENDING until policy approval (unchanged). **P2** — packaging extraction (independent follow-up; its corpus extension = the A5/A4(b)/A3-related probes become reviewer-labeled cases in a later corpus version with its own digest and label governance). **P3** — authority-side grounding (separate track). **Q3-C** — policy approval and qualification decision (unchanged in scope and sequence; 2.1 does not preempt it).
