## 2. Blocker A — Product identity versus specification alignment

### 2.1 The defect in Revision 1's G2/G3

Revision 1's U3/U4/U2N rules (its §5.2, §5.5) and the A1(ii) probe verdict treat **complete alignment of request-conveyed dimensions as "identity grounded."** That language is not automatically valid, for the reason the task states and the frozen corpus demonstrates concretely:

1. **Two distinct products may share every published specification.** The recorded catalog fixture (`tests/fixtures/pages/micron_7500_part_catalog.json`, re-read this session) contains **three distinct Micron 7500 4TB U.3 parts** — `MTFDKCC3T8TGP-1BK1DABYY`, `MTFDKCC3T8TGP-1BK1JABYY`, `MTFDKCC3T8TGP-1BK1DFCYY` — that share the prefix `MTFDKCC3T8TGP`, the part name "7500 4TB U.3 SSD", and the brand/family/capacity/form-factor description, and differ only in the unpublished tail (with a measurable commercial consequence: 6800 vs 7000 MB/s sequential-read grade). A "matching description" is therefore **not necessarily a verified manufacturer-part relationship** — the recorded world fact is that it is not one.
2. **The corpus itself is the proof case.** `V2Q-SSD-U4-AMBIG-0013` (U4, no candidate identifier; candidate = brand Micron + title "Micron 7500 4TB U.3 NVMe SSD"; target = `MTFDKCC3T8TGP-1BK1DABYY` / "Micron 7500 4TB U.3 NVMe SSD") is labeled **CONTESTABLE, expected UNCERTAIN**, acceptable set {UNCERTAIN, MATCH}, with the label evidence: *"The recorded catalog publishes THREE distinct 7500 4TB U.3 parts … the candidate publishes brand, family, capacity, and form factor but no identifier: the evidence cannot distinguish which of the three published parts is listed, so UNCERTAIN is independently supported. MATCH is defensible only by ignoring the recorded ambiguity."* Its twin `V2Q-SSD-U3-MATCH-0010` (identical product evidence **plus** the published prefix `MTFDKCC3T8TGP`) is labeled **UNAMBIGUOUS MATCH** — and the recorded catalog shows the prefix is shared by **all three** siblings, so the prefix does not resolve the sibling ambiguity either; it is *consistent with* it.
3. **What the two labels jointly establish:** the payload cannot distinguish "the description resolves to one product" from "the description resolves to many." The corpus's expected-pole difference between 0010 (MATCH) and 0013 (UNCERTAIN) is grounded in (a) the frozen U3/U4 state grading of a *published identifier claim* versus *no claim* (the S2-A requirement table caps U3 but not U4; both are decision-eligible) and (b) label-side catalog knowledge that is **not transmitted** through any frozen input channel. Neither label requires the model to *see* the sibling family; the 0013 label uses it to choose the expected pole; the 0010 label uses prefix *consistency* (true, but not unique) plus the frozen decision standard.

Revision 1 handled this only as risk R3/R10 ("a future event", "a documented residual") while its rules and its A1(ii) verdict still asserted that full alignment *grounds identity*. That is the defect: a prompt that tells the model "identity is grounded" on G2/G3 evidence licenses the model (and any downstream reader of the record) to treat the MATCH as product identification, when the evidence supports only **equivalence at the description's resolution** — and the frozen authority layer's substate grading (requirement caps, STRONG bar) is what actually encodes the trust difference.

### 2.2 The six evidence classes (definitions)

The corrected contract distinguishes, and never conflates, the following:

| # | Class | Definition (payload-internal) | What it grounds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Exact identifier evidence** | The exact requested MPN published with identity wording (MPN field, or title token without compatibility/reference/SEO wording — U1), or a published SKU the deterministic layer records as identical to the target (U2+SKU_EQUALS_TARGET) | The candidate's own published claim **is the requested part number**, at **part-number resolution**. (Residual: a listing can mislabel its part; the frozen contract accepts identifier claims at face value at the decision level — the same acceptance the deterministic layer applies to explicit MPN fields.) |
| 2 | **Reviewed manufacturer relation evidence** | A complete different part number in a bounded near-miss shape whose specific difference is explicitly established by `MANUFACTURER_RELATION_AUTHORITY` (labeled in CONTEXT PROVENANCE) — U5 | The **specific identifier relation** (e.g., 01/02 revision of the same part), at **part-number resolution**. Customer-retrieval relations and manufacturer product context never belong to this class (approved decisions 4, 5). |
| 3 | **Partial identifier evidence** | A candidate MPN that is a consistent truncation/prefix of the requested MPN (U3, frozen shape) | **Family membership**: the candidate claims to be a member of the requested part's number family. It does **not** ground the specific variant — the unpublished tail may encode a different variant (capacity, revision, grade, packaging), and the recorded catalog shows real prefixes shared by multiple siblings (§2.1). |
| 4 | **Attribute-only description alignment** | No usable candidate identifier (U4) or a different retailer SKU (U2+SKU_NOT_TARGET, which is neither a conflict nor a ground) plus alignment of the request-conveyed product dimensions | **Commercial equivalence at the resolution of the request's description**: "the same product the request describes, on everything the request describes." It does **not** ground product identification: the same description may be published by distinct products, and the payload does not show whether it is. |
| 5 | **Genuine product identity** | Verified identification of the candidate as the specific requested part | **Owned exclusively by the deterministic layer** (EXACT / NORMALIZED_EXACT → MACHINE_VERIFIED) **and by human confirmation** (HUMAN_CONFIRMED overlay). **No semantic-layer ground — including classes 1 and 2 — produces class 5**: a semantic MATCH is advisory at every resolution (frozen posture: "an LLM is never the sole authority for exact product identity"). |
| 6 | **Unresolved variant or catalog ambiguity** | The published evidence (or the labeler's recorded world) is consistent with two or more products | The state a ground **fails to resolve**. It may be payload-visible (a published contradiction, a raw cue, an internally contradictory pair) or **label-side only** (0013's recorded three-sibling family — not transmitted to the model, and the model is forbidden from guessing it). Where class 6 is not payload-visible, the contract's answer is the stated resolution plus the authority-layer grading — never a manufactured uniqueness claim and never a manufactured conflict. |

### 2.3 Identity resolution

Every MATCH ground carries exactly one **resolution**:

- **Part-number resolution** (classes 1, 2): the evidence grounds that the candidate *is the requested part number* (or the specifically related part the authority establishes). A MATCH at this resolution asserts the strongest advisory identity the semantic layer may carry.
- **Family+description resolution** (class 3 + full alignment): the evidence grounds that the candidate *claims the requested part's number family and matches everything the request describes*. A MATCH at this resolution does **not** assert that the candidate is the specific requested part number.
- **Description resolution** (class 4 + full alignment): the evidence grounds that the candidate *matches everything the request describes*. A MATCH at this resolution does **not** assert that the candidate is the specific requested part number, and does **not** assert that no other product shares the published description.

**No ground upgrades its resolution.** Alignment of attributes never upgrades a description-resolution ground into a part-number ground; a prefix never upgrades into the complete number it truncates. Resolution is fixed by the strongest identifier class present (class 2 where authority establishes a specific relation; else class 1; else class 3 where the consistent prefix is present; else class 4).

This is the correction to Revision 1: its IA-PARTIAL/IA-DISTINCT states and "identity is grounded" language are re-cast as **equivalence grounded at family+description / description resolution**. The frozen tier path's substate grading (requirement caps for U2N/U3; the dimension-A STRONG bar for U4; NOT_REQUIRED for U1) is recognized as the frozen authority expression of exactly these resolution differences — not as an incidental downstream detail.

### 2.4 What the frozen contract actually requires (verified, not assumed)

The determination "what evidence is sufficient for MATCH" must be made **under the frozen contract**, and the frozen contract was re-read this session:

1. **The frozen 2.0 decision standard is resolution-open and explicitly permits description grounds.** *"MATCH: the candidate is very likely the same commercially comparable product on the supplied evidence"*; *"a listing that publishes no usable MPN may still be the target product on title, description, and attribute evidence"*; *"Exact MPN equality is strong evidence but is not the only evidence of commercial equivalence."* (All verbatim, `SYSTEM_PROMPT_V2`, digest-pinned.)
2. **The frozen S2-A authority contract designs description-resolution MATCHes as real outcomes.** AD-066 (canonical §26.22): U4 + MATCH + HIGH + STRONG product evidence + clean conflicts ⇒ `AI_ASSISTED_COMPARABLE` **permitted** — the express purpose of the S2-A-FU1 correction was to stop re-conservatizing exactly the no-identifier description match. U2N/U3 carry `REVIEWED_RELATION_AUTHORITY_REQUIRED` (tier-capped, not decision-barred); U1 carries `NOT_REQUIRED`.
3. **The frozen identity-safety boundary is untouched by any of the above:** semantic MATCH is advisory; the LLM is never the sole authority for exact identity; deterministic code owns exact identity; human confirmation is the only part-identity overlay; NEEDS_REVIEW never prices; V2 is unqualified (persist-only).

**Determination:** under the frozen contract, description-level (and family+description-level) alignment **is** sufficient for a decision-level MATCH — at the stated resolution — and it **is not** sufficient for, and must not be represented as, unique product identification. A design that made full alignment *insufficient* for MATCH would contradict the frozen decision standard, AD-066's U4 intent, and six of the eleven frozen labels (a CORPUS_CONTRACT_CONFLICT on 0008/0010/0014/0017/0035/0039). A design that made full alignment *sufficient for unique identification* (Revision 1's implicit claim) would contradict the 0013 label and the frozen identity-safety boundary. The corrected contract occupies the only position consistent with all frozen text: **sufficient at the stated resolution; never sufficient for what the evidence does not carry.**

### 2.6 Reassessment of U2/SKU_NOT_TARGET, U3/PARTIAL_BOUNDARY, U4/NO_MPN

**U3 / PARTIAL_BOUNDARY (corrected).** A consistent prefix is (i) **not a conflicting full part number** (it cannot ground NO_MATCH on its own; the U5 entanglement does not apply — the gap is an unpublished tail of the *requested* identifier, not a candidate-side variant claim) and (ii) **not the complete number it truncates** — it grounds **family membership** (class 3), nothing more: the recorded catalog (§2.1) shows real family prefixes shared by multiple distinct parts. **U4 / NO_MPN (corrected).** No identifier question exists (frozen requirement NOT_APPLICABLE; preserved 2.0 rule: a missing candidate MPN is not automatically NO_MATCH). The decision rests entirely on the product evidence: full alignment → **G3, description resolution** (0014, 0017); generic overlap → U-1 UNCERTAIN (0015, 0043). The MATCH at this ground **does not assert product identification**: the same description may be published by distinct products (0013 is the recorded proof: three siblings, one description, no identifier — the label's expected pole is UNCERTAIN, and MATCH remains defensible by the frozen description-ground standard). ## 3. Blocker B — Sales-unit unknown and future authority

### 3.1 The six-way separation

The task's six concepts, with their owners under the frozen system:

| # | Concept | Owner | What it is / is not |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Physical product identity** | Deterministic layer (exact/normalized MPN) + semantic layer (advisory grounds, §2.3 resolutions) + human confirmation (overlay) | Is the candidate the same physical product as the requested part? Grounded at part-number / family+description / description resolution. Never produced as *verified* identity by the semantic layer alone. |
| 2 | **Commercial sales-unit equivalence** | Published packaging evidence only (structured OBSERVED channel, or clear raw cue, read under the COMMERCIAL SALES-UNIT RULES) | Is the candidate sold in the same commercial unit as the target's (single unit of the requested part, unless target-side supplied evidence establishes otherwise)? Grounded **only** by positive published evidence (SU-EQ). **Never** by absence: `UNAVAILABLE` is a recorded absence ("do not infer a single unit" — frozen text). |
| 3 | **Price comparability** | The conjunction of (1) and (2) | May the candidate's price be aggregated with the target's? Requires the same physical product **and** the same commercial unit. Frozen 2.0: *"The same underlying physical product sold with a different pack quantity, sales unit, bundle, or as an accessory … is NOT pricing-comparable as the same sales unit."* A MATCH with an unknown unit is an identity claim **without** a price-comparability claim. |
| 4 | **Semantic decision** | The model (bounded output: decision + confidence + reason code + structured attribute lists + conflict classes) | The only model surface. It can *name* packaging in the three lists (proven equal / proven different / unproven-and-decision-critical) or stay silent on it ("not determined" — §8). |
| 5 | **Derived tier** | Frozen S2-A `derive_authority_tier` (deterministic) | `f(decision, confidence, dimension-A product evidence quality, structured conflict classes, substate relationship requirement, context provenances)` → tier + fired rules. **Does not see (2)** — §3.3. |
| 6 | **Actual pricing eligibility** | Tier ∉ the gate | A candidate's evidence enters pricing output only if its derived tier ∈ `PRICING_ELIGIBLE_TIERS` = {MACHINE_VERIFIED, AI_ASSISTED_COMPARABLE, HUMAN_CONFIRMED} **and** the V2 route is qualified (`V2_AUTHORITY_QUALIFIED`) under an approved policy with the eight gates passing. **Today: always False for every V2 record** (persist-only). |

The chain is one-way: (4) → (5) → (6), with (1) and (2) as the evidence questions (4) answers. No backflow: the tier never re-decides the evidence questions, and the model never sees the tier.

### 3.2 The meaning of `MATCH + sales_unit=UNAVAILABLE` (corrected)

The frozen output schema has **no sales-unit assertion field**. The four expressible positions are unchanged from Revision 1 §3 (proven equal → `matched_attributes`; proven different → `conflicting_attributes` + ALWAYS_HARD class → NO_MATCH; unproven-and-decision-critical → `missing_critical_attributes` → UNCERTAIN; **not determined → absent from all three lists**).

An advisory MATCH with `sales_unit=UNAVAILABLE` and no published cue (SU-UNK) asserts **exactly**:

1. **Physical product identity at the resolution of its strongest ground** (§2.3): part-number (G1/G2-auth), family+description (G2-partial), or description (G3) — never more.
2. **No published evidence shows a different commercial unit** (no SU-X observation, no clear raw cue).

It asserts **not at all**: single-unit status; equal pack quantity; tray/retail equivalence; **price comparability**. The price-comparability question (concept 3) is **OPEN** — it is not decided by the MATCH, and it is not decided by the absence of packaging evidence. `UNAVAILABLE` is a recorded absence, and the absence of PACKAGING_QUANTITY/BUNDLE from all three lists in a MATCH response means "not determined on the supplied evidence," which the model, the harness, downstream code, and human readers must not read as "proven single unit," "proven equal unit," **or "pricing-comparable."** Only a human confirmation (HUMAN_CONFIRMED overlay) or a future reviewed/deterministic packaging observation closes the question.

This corrects Revision 1 §3, which stated the two assertions correctly but did not state the price-comparability non-assertion — the omission that let "MATCH" do double duty as a (3) claim in downstream reasoning. The prompt clause is corrected in §9.2 ("WHAT A MATCH ASSERTS, PRECISELY").

### 3.3 Does the frozen tier derivation distinguish SU-EQ from SU-UNK? **No — the architectural limitation, stated**

**Determination (source-verified this session):** the existing frozen tier derivation does **not** have sufficient information to distinguish a MATCH with proven equal sales unit from a MATCH with unknown sales unit. It is **structurally sales-unit-blind**:

1. `derive_authority_tier`'s inputs are `assessment_v2` (state/substate/signals/normalized keys), `evaluation: SemanticEvaluationV2` = **(decision, confidence, conflict_classes)** only, `context_provenances`, `product_evidence` (the authority-side profile: usable title + grounded matched facts), and `human_review`. The model's `matched_attributes` / `missing_critical_attributes` — the only surfaces where the sales-unit state is expressed (§8) — are **not inputs**. The input case's `sales_unit` channel (UNAVAILABLE/OBSERVED) is rendered into the prompt and then consumed by **no** authority symbol.
2. The 27-entry `SEMANTIC_OUTCOME_TIER_MATRIX` is keyed on (decision × confidence × product-evidence quality); the ceilings (reviewable conflict, NM-2-without-authority, requirement-without-established-relation) and the HARD_CONFLICT supersession consume only conflict classes, provenances, and the requirement table. Packaging reaches the derivation **only negatively**: a published incompatible unit produces `PACKAGING_QUANTITY`/`BUNDLE` conflict classes on a NO_MATCH → `HARD_CONFLICT_SUPERSEDES` → EXCLUDED.
3. **Consequence (provable in the frozen code):** a SU-EQ MATCH (packaging reported in `matched_attributes`) and a SU-UNK MATCH (packaging absent from all lists), with identical decision, confidence, quality, conflicts, substate, and provenances, derive **identical tiers and identical pricing eligibility**. This identity is pinned as a T2 test property in §10.3.

This is not a defect of 2.1 and not something a prompt can fix: it is a property of the frozen S2-A authority contract. **No prompt wording, qualification gate, or matrix row can make the derivation sales-unit-aware.** The only structural paths are (i) making the unit *observable* (P2 packaging extraction: SU-UNK collapses into SU-EQ/SU-X where pages publish packaging), (ii) adding a sales-unit channel to the authority derivation (P4, §3.6 — a frozen-contract amendment), or (iii) withholding qualification (the current state, §3.4).

## 4. Corrected identity-sufficiency rules (decision semantics, consolidated)

"Request-conveyed hard identity dimension" = a hard identity dimension (PRODUCT_FAMILY, GENERATION, CAPACITY, INTERFACE, FORM_FACTOR, PRODUCT_ROLE, ACCESSORY_RELATION) whose value the target's MPN description publishes. "Pinned" = the candidate side publishes a value for that dimension matching the conveyed value under normalized equivalence ("3.84 Terabytes" = "3.84TB"; "U.3 2.5in" pins U.3 with consistent additional detail), credited from structured facts **and** raw observation text (credit-all-first). "Strictly coarser" = the candidate publishes a value that does not contradict but does not pin the conveyed value ("DDR5" vs conveyed "DDR5-6400" — the coarser token pins nothing).

### 4.1 U1_TITLE_MPN

The exact requested MPN token in the title is **identity wording** unless the surrounding wording is compatibility / reference / SEO ("compatible with", "replacement for", "drop-in", "upgrade from", "fast upgrade from X"): then it is not an identifier ground (the published product role decides — an accessory is N-2). With identity wording: **G1, part-number resolution** (M-1). A bare token (nothing else published, 0005) is identity wording (no marker present); attribute gaps are **not** decision-critical with G1 (§6.1 part 3) — MATCH is the contract default, UNCERTAIN remains defensible (label CONTESTABLE; watch R13). A published conflict on any identity dimension still decides N-2 regardless of the exact identifier (0003 CAPACITY, 0004 GENERATION, 0031 ACCESSORY).

### 4.2 U2_SKU_ONLY

**SKU_EQUALS_TARGET:** the published SKU is frozen-2A identical to the target (the deterministic comparator is the authority): **G1, part-number resolution** (M-1). The identifier ground never overrides a published product-role conflict (0007: SKU equal, product is an adapter cable → N-2).

**SKU_NOT_TARGET:** a different retailer SKU is **not a conflict** (never grounds NO_MATCH, never enters the conflict set — retailer SKUs are not manufacturer identifiers; frozen 2.0 rule preserved) and **not an identifier ground** (absence of conflict is not proof of equivalence). It contributes no resolution. The decision rests entirely on the product evidence: full alignment → **G3, description resolution** (M-4; 0008, 0035, 0039); generic overlap → U-1 UNCERTAIN naming the unpinned dimension(s) (0009). The rendered requirement (`REVIEWED_RELATION_AUTHORITY_REQUIRED`) is downstream tier policy (§4.7): it caps the derived tier, it does not gate the decision, and it is the frozen authority expression of "a published identifier-like claim that is not the target is not trusted for automatic pricing."

### 4.3 U3_PARTIAL_BOUNDARY

The candidate's published MPN is a **consistent truncation/prefix** of the requested MPN (frozen shape; the prefix property is established, not re-litigated). The corrected semantics, in full:

- A consistent prefix is **not a conflicting full part number**: the candidate did not publish a different complete number; the prefix cannot ground NO_MATCH on its own, and the U5 entanglement (SU-ENT) does **not** apply — the gap is an unpublished tail of the *requested* identifier, not a candidate-side variant claim.
- A consistent prefix is **not the complete number it truncates**: it grounds **family membership** (class 3) — the candidate claims the requested part's number family. It does **not** ground the specific variant: the unpublished tail may encode a different variant (capacity, revision, grade, packaging), and real family prefixes are shared by distinct parts (the recorded catalog's three 4TB siblings, §2.1 — label-side fact, not transmitted, not relied upon by the model).
- **Sufficient positive evidence** — the prefix + product evidence grounds **G2-partial, family+description resolution** (M-3) when, and only when, all three hold, **each testable on the supplied evidence alone** (no catalog context is required or permitted):
  - **(U3-a)** the frozen shape is U3_PARTIAL_BOUNDARY (consistent prefix; established fact);
  - **(U3-b)** **every request-conveyed hard identity dimension is pinned** on the candidate side (normalized equivalence; credit-all-first over structured facts *and* raw title/specification text);
  - **(U3-c)** **no supplied evidence indicates a different variant**: no raw packaging/revision/bundle cue, no published contradiction, no unresolved published reviewable difference, no internally contradictory request-conveyed dimension.
- The MATCH at this ground **asserts family+description resolution only**: the candidate is the same commercially comparable product the request describes, within the claimed family — it does **not** assert that the candidate is the specific requested part number, and the specific-variant question remains open (the frozen U3 requirement cap, §4.7, withholds automatic pricing for exactly that reason).
- Where (U3-b) fails, the unpublished tail *may carry* the difference for exactly the unpinned dimensions: they are decision-critical → **U-1** (UNCERTAIN naming them; 0012: prefix + "NVMe data center SSD" vs requested "1.92TB U.2" → CAPACITY, FORM_FACTOR unpinned). Where a published contradiction exists → **N-2** (0011: prefix + "8TB" vs requested 4TB → NO_MATCH CAPACITY; the prefix is irrelevant to a published conflict).

**Anchor (0010):** requested `MTFDKCC3T8TGP-1BK1DABYY` / "Micron 7500 4TB U.3 NVMe SSD"; candidate publishes MPN `MTFDKCC3T8TGP` + brand Micron (structured) + "Micron 7500 4TB U.3 NVMe SSD" (title). (U3-a) frozen shape ✔; (U3-b) every request-conveyed hard dimension pinned: BRAND (Micron) ✔, PRODUCT_FAMILY (7500/SSD) ✔, CAPACITY (4TB) ✔, FORM_FACTOR (U.3) ✔, INTERFACE (NVMe) ✔; (U3-c) no variant cue, no contradiction ✔ → **G2-partial, family+description resolution** → **MATCH**. The specific variant (…DABYY vs …JABYY vs …DFCYY) is **not** grounded by the payload — the recorded catalog shows all three siblings share the prefix and the description (§2.1, verified this session; label-side, RC-3, not transmitted, not relied upon). The label's MATCH is supportable at the stated resolution under the frozen decision standard (§2.4), with the frozen U3 requirement cap as the authority expression of the open variant question.

### 4.4 U4_NO_MPN

No usable candidate identifier; the identifier question does not exist (frozen requirement NOT_APPLICABLE — there is nothing to establish; preserved 2.0 rule: a missing candidate MPN is not automatically NO_MATCH). The decision rests entirely on the product evidence:

- **Full alignment (G3, description resolution, M-4):** every request-conveyed hard identity dimension is pinned on the candidate side, no supplied evidence indicates a different product or variant, and no published identity conflict (hard or reviewable) exists. The MATCH **asserts description resolution only**: the candidate is the same commercially comparable product the request describes, on everything the request describes — it does **not** assert that the candidate is the specific requested part number, and it does **not** assert that the description resolves to a single product in the world (alignment is not uniqueness, §4.8). Anchors: 0014 ("96GB DDR5-6400 MRDIMM Server Memory Module" pins 96GB / DDR5-6400 / MRDIMM / server memory — the request's full conveyed spec set); 0017 ("3.84TB U.3 NVMe SSD, data center" pins 3.84TB / U.3 / NVMe / data-center; the only published difference is CONDITION Refurbished vs new — price-only, may be reported, never blocks).
- **Generic overlap (U-1):** the candidate publishes only a subset of the request-conveyed hard dimensions, or a strictly coarser value for one of them — a different product or variant remains consistent with everything published; description resolution is **not** grounded → UNCERTAIN naming the unpinned dimension(s). ### 4.5 U5_NEAR_MISS_MPN (NM-1 truncation / NM-2 substitution)

The candidate publishes a **COMPLETE** part number different from the requested one in a bounded near-miss shape (frozen: NEAR_MISS_TRUNCATION / NEAR_MISS_SUBSTITUTION). Corrected semantics:

1. **Never automatically equivalent** (frozen 2.0 rule preserved). A complete different number is itself published identifier evidence: it **blocks distinctive alignment** — aligned product attributes cannot override the candidate's published claim to be a specific different number (anchors 0018/0022: full attribute alignment, yet not MATCH).
2. **Equivalence is established ONLY** by `MANUFACTURER_RELATION_AUTHORITY` explicitly establishing the specific relationship (or manufacturer-stated evidence resolving the difference as not material) — **G2-auth, part-number resolution** for the specifically related part. A customer-retrieval relation and a manufacturer product context **never** establish it (approved decisions 4, 5). Established → M-2 (reason MATCH_AUTHORIZED_IDENTIFIER_RELATION; the resolved difference is not reported as a conflicting revision). Anchor 0020.
3. **Not established:** the difference is either *resolved-different* (the supplied evidence shows it corresponds to an actual product difference — then N-3 where the number itself is the judged conflict, or N-2 where published hard conflicts decide as in 0019: CAPACITY + INTERFACE published-conflicting alongside the number shape) or *unproven-different* (the difference's meaning — revision, packaging, sales unit, variant — is not established on the supplied evidence). Asserting `MPN_IDENTITY` requires the resolved-different reading; the unproven-different reading is UNCERTAIN, never a manufactured conflict.
4. **SU-ENT (row U-2):** where the unproven difference *may carry the sales-unit/packaging/revision question* — the unpublished suffix could denote a packaging or sales-unit variant and no manufacturer-stated evidence resolves it — the missing sales unit is decision-critical: **UNCERTAIN naming PACKAGING_QUANTITY** (and/or BUNDLE). Anchors 0018/0022 (the R/T suffix; customer-retrieval-only or no relationship; the sales-unit-safety flag: a MATCH here is the CRITICAL false MATCH the case exists to catch). The customer-retrieval relation makes the variant question concrete (the alias says the pair is related for retrieval) while conferring **zero** equivalence authority — it is never described to the model as manufacturer equivalence, and it never licenses MATCH.
5. **Revision-shaped (row U-3):** 0021/0023 (01/02 substitution, identical titles, no sales-unit-safety flag, no authority / product-context-only): the difference may carry a REVISION question (reviewable, resolvable by inspection) → default **UNCERTAIN**; the NO_MATCH pole is permitted where the model judges the published difference to establish a different product (label CONTESTABLE {NO_MATCH, UNCERTAIN}); MATCH is excluded on both poles (row precedence: not G2-auth, distinctive alignment blocked by rule 1).

### 4.6 The "Relationship requirement" line (approved decision 3, preserved — with its corrected reading)

The rendered line is **downstream authority-tier policy**: it records whether the candidate's derived pricing-authority tier requires a reviewed relationship source to be ESTABLISHED. It is **not** the model's decision input. It neither blocks nor permits a MATCH. Its corrected reading under Blocker A: the frozen requirement table is the authority layer's expression of **ground trust by identifier shape** — `REVIEWED_RELATION_AUTHORITY_REQUIRED` marks the shapes whose published identifier claims are unresolved (U2N: the SKU is not the target; U3: the prefix is not the complete number; U5: the different number is unestablished), so their MATCHes are tier-capped at NEEDS_REVIEW (not pricing-eligible) until a reviewed relation answers the specific claim; `NOT_REQUIRED` (U1) and `NOT_APPLICABLE` (U2E: deterministically answered; U4: no claim exists) mark the shapes where the automatic tier is gated only by the dimension-A evidence bar and the ceilings. The two-line user-template annotation (§9.4) is unchanged from Revision 1.

### 4.7 The DISTINCTIVE-ALIGNMENT TEST (single definition, used by U2N / U3 / U4)

The product evidence **distinctly aligns** the candidate with the requested product when:

1. **Pinning:** every hard identity dimension the target request conveys through its MPN description is pinned on the candidate side — a published matching value (normalized equivalence), credited from structured facts **and** raw observation text (credit-all-first: "credit ALL supplied evidence on both sides before judging; a value published on the candidate and uncontradicted is NOT missing").
2. **No variant indication:** no supplied evidence (either side, structured or raw) indicates a different product or variant — no published contradiction, no raw packaging/revision/bundle cue, no unresolved published reviewable difference, no internally contradictory request-conveyed dimension.
3. **Bounded by supplied evidence:** the test is judged only on what is published on the two sides. The model does not use catalog knowledge or outside product knowledge to imagine unpublished variants ("Do not rely on unstated specifications, catalog knowledge, or outside product knowledge" — frozen 2.0, preserved).
4. **Alignment is not uniqueness (new, Blocker A):** pinning every request-conveyed dimension makes the candidate a strong match **at the description's resolution**. It does **not** establish that the description resolves to a single product in the market, that the requested part has no sibling variants, or that the candidate is the specific requested part number — none of those is shown by the payload, and the test is never read as showing them. The resolution of the resulting ground is fixed by the identifier class present (§2.3): family+description with a consistent prefix (U3); description without one (U2N/U4).

A dimension the request does **not** convey is never decision-critical for identity and is never named missing (fixes the 0035 BRAND/PRODUCT_FAMILY mis-crediting direction and the 0017 BRAND/GENERATION/REVISION_OR_SUFFIX, 0039 FORM_FACTOR over-demands). A value published by the candidate but not conveyed by the request is credited, not required (0039 MRDIMM; 0035 "PCIe Gen4"; 0014 brand).

## 6. Corrected missing-critical and contradiction semantics (preserved from Revision 1, part 3 re-anchored)

### 6.1 The three-part definition

A dimension D belongs in `missing_critical_attributes` **only if all three hold**:

1. **ELIGIBLE.** D is a **request-conveyed** hard identity dimension (PRODUCT_FAMILY, GENERATION, CAPACITY, INTERFACE, FORM_FACTOR, PRODUCT_ROLE, ACCESSORY_RELATION), or D is PACKAGING_QUANTITY / BUNDLE under the SU-ENT condition (row U-2). Consequences: a dimension the request does not convey is **never** decision-critical and never named (0017 BRAND/GENERATION/REVISION_OR_SUFFIX; 0039 FORM_FACTOR); BRAND, REVISION_OR_SUFFIX, and CONDITION are **never** named missing (a published difference on them is a conflicting attribute or an authority-resolved relation; an unpublished one is resolvable by inspection — the frozen tier path for reviewable matters).
2. **UNPINNED.** The candidate side publishes **no pinning value** for D: no matching value and no strictly-coarser value (the coarser token pins nothing — 0009 "DDR5" vs "DDR5-6400"). A value published on the candidate and uncontradicted is **credited, not missing** (credit-all-first; fixes the 0035 PRODUCT_FAMILY under-credit). A published **contradiction** against the target is a **conflict** (N-2/N-3), not a missing attribute. A **self-contradictory** published pair (0037) leaves D **unpinned in both directions** (approved decision 7) — neither side may be adopted, so D is unpinned, not contradicted-against-the-target.
3. **DECISION-CRITICAL.** The decision **depends** on D: the supplied evidence grounds equivalence (at any resolution, §2.3) **only with** D pinned. Operationally: with a part-number-resolution ground (G1/G2-auth) attribute gaps are **not** decision-critical — the exact part number (or the authority-established relation) is the ground and an unpublished attribute is resolvable by inspection (0005 stays MATCH-default without naming anything); with a family+description (G2-partial) or description (G3) ground, the alignment test **fails on** the unpinned dimension(s) — they are precisely the gaps (0012, 0015, 0043, 0009); under SU-ENT the packaging gap is decision-critical by definition (0018, 0022). **Re-anchoring note (Blocker A):** criticality is defined against the *grounding at the stated resolution*, not against product identification — an unpinned request-conveyed dimension defeats even description resolution, which is why it is decision-critical, and no dimension beyond the request-conveyed set is ever named.

**The credit-all-first rule** (preserved): "Credit ALL supplied evidence on both sides (structured facts and raw observation text) BEFORE naming anything missing."

### 6.2 Consequences for the universal 2.0 pattern (preserved)

The 11/11 universal `PACKAGING_QUANTITY` abstention (all inputs SU-UNK, none SU-ENT) is removed: SU-UNK with grounded equivalence is row M-*, where PACKAGING_QUANTITY/BUNDLE are **not eligible** (part 1) and **not unpinned-as-decision-critical** (§3.2: the absence means "not determined", and the open price-comparability question is not a missing *identity* attribute). The only SU-UNK cases that name PACKAGING_QUANTITY are the SU-ENT ones (0018/0022) — the label-pinned set.

### 6.3 Internal contradiction (approved decision 7, axis-clarified — preserved)

If the supplied evidence contradicts **itself** on a dimension (title vs specification text; one published field vs another), that dimension is **unpinned in both directions**: it is neither established nor contradicted *against the target*. No published side may be adopted as the product's value; the corresponding conflict class is never asserted; NO_MATCH is never returned on the contradiction alone. If the dimension is request-conveyed and the contradiction leaves it unpinned, it is decision-critical (part 3, via grounding failure) → **U-5**: UNCERTAIN naming the dimension. Anchor 0037: CAPACITY (title 3.84TB vs spec 7.68TB) → UNCERTAIN naming CAPACITY; the 2.0 capture's NO_MATCH/CAPACITY was the false-NO_MATCH the rule removes (label already expects UNCERTAIN — no label change needed or permitted). A self-contradictory **packaging** pair (title "single unit" vs spec "tray of 20") is the packaging-dimension instance of the same rule: the cue is unresolvable → U-4-style UNCERTAIN, never NO_MATCH on the contradiction alone (the absolute rule N-1 requires a *published*, i.e., externally consistent, incompatibility).

You are a product commercial-equivalence evaluation assistant.

TASK   [2.0 unchanged]
Your task is to determine the COMMERCIAL SEMANTIC EQUIVALENCE of a TARGET product and a CANDIDATE listing: does the candidate listing represent the same commercially comparable product as the target, and with what confidence?

You are given a structured input with five sections: TARGET, CANDIDATE LISTING, DETERMINISTIC IDENTITY CONTEXT, CONTEXT PROVENANCE, and PRODUCT EVIDENCE. WHAT YOU ARE NOT ASKED TO DO   [2.0 unchanged]
- Do not estimate, infer, or compare prices. Price and package observations are supplied as commercial context only and are NEVER identity evidence.
- Do not decide or override deterministic identity status. The deterministic layer has already established the DETERMINISTIC IDENTITY CONTEXT; you cannot make a candidate "machine verified", and you cannot override a deterministic hard conflict.

WHAT YOU SHOULD DETERMINE   [2.1 CHANGED (R1, revised R2): the questions are made explicit, with resolution and price comparability stated]
- First, whether the candidate is the same commercially comparable product as the target on the supplied evidence, and at what RESOLUTION the identification is grounded — part-number, family+description, or description (IDENTIFIER RELATIONSHIP RULES, DISTINCTIVE-ALIGNMENT TEST, and IDENTITY RESOLUTION). A MATCH at family or description resolution is not a claim of part-number identity, and a MATCH with an unproven sales unit is not a claim of price comparability.
- Which bounded attributes match, which conflict, and which critical attributes are missing.

- Whether uncertainty remains, and what would resolve it.

EVIDENCE CLASSES (how to read the input)   [2.0 unchanged, except one [2.1 NEW] sentence in the COMMERCIAL class, preserved from revisions 0/1]
- STRUCTURED FACT: a bounded value with an explicit source label (published structured field, listing title, specification text, or reviewed manufacturer product context). A structured fact is an OBSERVATION about one side of the comparison: it may support your reasoning, but it is not by itself a proof that the two sides agree. Equivalence between the sides is your semantic judgment.
- RAW OBSERVATION TEXT: free text as published (the listing title, the requested description). Raw text may support semantic reasoning, but a token occurring in raw text is NOT a structured fact, and raw text NEVER becomes manufacturer authority. Absence of a structured fact does not erase the raw observation evidence; the absence of both is not a value.

IDENTIFIER RELATIONSHIP RULES   [2.1 NEW (R1, revised R2); replaces and extends "IDENTIFIER EVIDENCE RULES"]
- The DETERMINISTIC IDENTITY CONTEXT has already established the identifier shape (state, substate, signals, normalized keys). Do not re-litigate the shape; apply the rule for the substate it records.
- An identifier relationship is ESTABLISHED only by: (i) the exact requested MPN published with identity wording (MPN field, or the exact token in the title without compatibility / reference / SEO wording); (ii) a published SKU that the deterministic layer records as identical to the target; or (iii) a complete different part number in a bounded near-miss shape, where the CONTEXT PROVENANCE explicitly lists MANUFACTURER_RELATION_AUTHORITY establishing that relationship. Nothing else establishes it: a different retailer SKU, a consistent partial form of the requested identifier, a customer-retrieval relation, and a manufacturer product context never establish it by themselves.
- An established identifier relationship is strong evidence but is not the only evidence of commercial equivalence: a published conflict on an identity dimension (hard or reviewable) still decides the outcome — a candidate identified as the requested part that is published to be a cable, a caddy, a tray, or a different capacity is a NO_MATCH.
- A missing candidate MPN is not automatically NO_MATCH: a listing that publishes no usable MPN may still be the target product on title, description, and attribute evidence. No identifier question exists for such a listing; decide on the product evidence.
- A target MPN appearing in the listing title may be product identity OR compatibility / reference / SEO wording (for example "compatible with", "replacement for", "drop-in", "upgrade from"). Inspect the wording context before treating title MPN text as identity evidence. Compatibility / reference / SEO wording does not establish the identifier relationship; the published product role then decides (an accessory is a NO_MATCH).
- U2, a different retailer SKU (SKU_NOT_TARGET): a different SKU is NOT a conflict and NOT proof of a different product (retailer SKUs are not manufacturer identifiers). It is also NOT an identifier ground: the absence of a conflict is not proof of equivalence. It contributes no resolution. The decision rests entirely on the product evidence under the DISTINCTIVE-ALIGNMENT TEST, at DESCRIPTION resolution (see U4 and IDENTITY RESOLUTION).
- U3, a consistent partial form (PARTIAL_BOUNDARY): a candidate MPN that is a consistent truncation / prefix of the requested MPN is a PARTIAL FORM of the requested identifier: it is NOT a different published part number and NOT a conflicting revision. It grounds the candidate's claim of FAMILY MEMBERSHIP in the requested part's number family — NOT the specific variant: the unpublished tail may encode a different variant (capacity, revision, grade, packaging) that the published description does not distinguish, and a family prefix can be shared by more than one part. When the DISTINCTIVE-ALIGNMENT TEST is satisfied, the candidate is grounded at FAMILY+DESCRIPTION resolution (see IDENTITY RESOLUTION): a MATCH on this ground asserts the same commercially comparable product the request describes, within the claimed family, and does NOT assert that the candidate is the specific requested part number. Where the test fails, the unpublished tail may carry the difference for exactly the unpinned dimensions: they are decision-critical (UNCERTAIN, naming them) — never a NO_MATCH on the prefix alone.
- U4, no usable candidate identifier (NO_RELATION): no identifier question exists. The decision rests entirely on the product evidence under the DISTINCTIVE-ALIGNMENT TEST, at DESCRIPTION resolution: the same description can be published by different products, and the supplied evidence does not show that it is not here. A MATCH on this ground asserts the same commercially comparable product the request describes, on everything the request describes, and does NOT assert that the candidate is the specific requested part number.
- U5, a complete different part number (NEAR_MISS_MPN: truncation of the requested form, or exactly one character substituted): a complete different published part number is NOT automatically equivalent, and it is itself published identifier evidence: it BLOCKS the DISTINCTIVE-ALIGNMENT TEST — aligned product attributes cannot override the candidate's published claim to be a specific different number. Equivalence is established ONLY by MANUFACTURER_RELATION_AUTHORITY explicitly establishing the specific relationship (or manufacturer-stated evidence resolving the difference as not material) — a MATCH on this ground is at PART-NUMBER resolution for the specifically related part; a customer-retrieval relation and a manufacturer product context never establish it. When the relationship is not established: the decision is NO_MATCH only where the supplied evidence shows the difference resolves to an actual product difference (assert the corresponding conflict classes), or UNCERTAIN where the difference's meaning (revision, packaging, sales unit, variant) is unproven on the supplied evidence — do not assert MPN_IDENTITY on an unproven difference. Where the unproven difference may itself carry the sales-unit / packaging / revision question, the COMMERCIAL SALES-UNIT RULES (MISSING SALES UNIT, CRITICAL) apply and the safe definite output is UNCERTAIN. When MANUFACTURER_RELATION_AUTHORITY establishes the relationship, do not report the resolved difference as a conflicting revision.

IDENTITY RESOLUTION   [2.1 NEW (R2)]
- A MATCH grounds the candidate at the resolution of its strongest identifier ground, and asserts nothing beyond that resolution:
  * PART-NUMBER resolution: the exact requested MPN published with identity wording, or a published SKU the deterministic layer records as identical to the target, or a complete different part number whose specific difference MANUFACTURER_RELATION_AUTHORITY establishes.
  * FAMILY+DESCRIPTION resolution: a consistent partial form of the requested identifier (U3) plus alignment of every hard identity dimension the request conveys.
  * DESCRIPTION resolution: no candidate identifier (U4) or a different retailer SKU (U2) plus alignment of every hard identity dimension the request conveys.
- A MATCH at family or description resolution asserts that the candidate is the same commercially comparable product the request describes, on everything the request describes. It does NOT assert that the candidate is the specific requested part number, and it does NOT assert that no other product shares the published description or family.
- Exact product identity is owned by the deterministic layer and by human confirmation: your MATCH at any resolution is advisory commercial equivalence, not verified identity, and alignment of attributes never upgrades the resolution — description alignment does not become a part-number ground, and a prefix does not become the complete number it truncates.

DISTINCTIVE-ALIGNMENT TEST   [2.1 NEW (R1, revised R2)]
- The product evidence DISTINCTLY ALIGNS the candidate with the requested product when: (1) every hard identity dimension (PRODUCT_FAMILY, GENERATION, CAPACITY, INTERFACE, FORM_FACTOR, PRODUCT_ROLE) that the target request conveys through its MPN description is PINNED on the candidate side — the candidate publishes a value matching the conveyed value under normalized equivalence, credited from structured facts AND raw observation text (credit ALL supplied evidence on both sides before judging); (2) no supplied evidence indicates a different product or variant — no published contradiction, no raw packaging / revision / bundle cue, no unresolved published reviewable difference, no internally contradictory request-conveyed dimension; and (3) the test is judged only on the SUPPLIED EVIDENCE — do not use catalog knowledge or outside product knowledge to imagine unpublished variants.
- Alignment is NOT distinctive (GENERIC OVERLAP) when at least one request-conveyed hard identity dimension is not pinned: the candidate publishes no value for it, or publishes a value strictly coarser than the conveyed one (for example "DDR5" where the request conveys "DDR5-6400": the coarser token does not contradict, but it does not pin the speed). Generic overlap leaves a different product or variant consistent with everything published; no resolution is grounded, and the unpinned dimensions are then decision-critical.
- Alignment is NOT uniqueness: pinning every request-conveyed dimension makes the candidate a strong match at the description's resolution; it does not establish that the description resolves to a single product in the market, and the test is never read as doing so.
- A value published on the candidate side that is not conveyed by the request and not contradicted is credited (it may strengthen the match) but is not required. A published contradiction on any request-conveyed hard dimension is a conflict and decides the outcome regardless of alignment elsewhere. A dimension the request does not convey is not decision-critical for identity (never name it missing).

READING THE DETERMINISTIC IDENTITY CONTEXT   [2.1 NEW (R1, revised R2)]
- The identifier facts in this section are established by the frozen deterministic layer; do not re-litigate them.
- The "Relationship requirement" line is DOWNSTREAM AUTHORITY-TIER POLICY: it records whether the candidate's derived pricing-authority tier requires a reviewed relationship source to be established. It is NOT your decision input. It neither blocks nor permits a MATCH: it marks which published identifier claims the authority layer does not yet trust for automatic pricing — a MATCH on the supplied evidence is possible even where the requirement is REVIEWED_RELATION_AUTHORITY_REQUIRED (the requirement then caps a derived tier you cannot see), and a NO_MATCH or UNCERTAIN is required where the evidence does not support equivalence even though the requirement is NOT_REQUIRED or NOT_APPLICABLE. Decide solely per the IDENTIFIER RELATIONSHIP RULES, the IDENTITY RESOLUTION, the COMMERCIAL SALES-UNIT RULES, the MISSING-CRITICAL ATTRIBUTES RULES, and the DECISION RULES.

CONTEXT PROVENANCE RULES   [2.1 CHANGED (preserved from revisions 0/1: all 2.0 text retained; the first two bullets each gain one appended sentence)]
- CUSTOMER_RETRIEVAL_RELATION, when present, is a RETRIEVAL HINT ONLY: it explains how the candidate was found. It is NOT manufacturer equivalence, it is NOT identity authority, and it confers no product authority. Use it as a retrieval hint only. Where the only relationship between two different published part numbers is a customer-retrieval relation, the identifier relationship is NOT established.
- MANUFACTURER_RELATION_AUTHORITY, when explicitly present, is stronger evidence: a reviewed authoritative source explicitly establishes the relevant identifier relationship. Use it as labeled manufacturer relationship authority, not as a guess. When it establishes a near-miss relationship, do not report that resolved difference as a conflicting revision.
- MANUFACTURER_PRODUCT_CONTEXT, when present, is reviewed product grounding about the base product; it strengthens product evidence but does not establish the identifier relationship.

PRODUCT EVIDENCE RULES   [2.0 unchanged]
- The PRODUCT EVIDENCE section is authority-side evidence produced by deterministic or reviewed sources. You may observe it and report semantic conclusions, but you must NOT create, upgrade, or assert new product evidence: your matched_attributes are conclusions about equivalence, not new authority-side facts.

COMMERCIAL SALES-UNIT RULES   [2.1 CHANGED (R1, revised R2); replaces "PHYSICAL PRODUCT vs COMMERCIAL SALES UNIT"]
- Distinguish PHYSICAL-PRODUCT equivalence from COMMERCIAL SALES-UNIT / PACKAGING equivalence. The same underlying physical product sold with a different pack quantity, sales unit, bundle, or as an accessory (tray, caddy, enclosure) is NOT pricing-comparable as the same sales unit.
- The target's commercial unit is the single unit of the requested part, unless the supplied target-side evidence (the requested description, or a reviewed target context) explicitly establishes a different form.
- WHAT A MATCH ASSERTS, PRECISELY: a MATCH asserts (1) that the candidate is the same commercially comparable product as the target, grounded on the supplied evidence at the resolution stated by IDENTITY RESOLUTION — part-number, family+description, or description, never more — and (2) that NO PUBLISHED evidence shows the candidate sold in a different commercial sales unit. A MATCH does NOT assert price comparability of the commercial sales unit unless published packaging evidence establishes the same commercial unit (report it in matched_attributes where it does). When the sales-unit channel is UNAVAILABLE and no packaging cue is published, a MATCH does NOT assert that the sales units are equal and does NOT assert a single unit: the sales-unit question is UNPROVEN and is not claimed in either direction, and the price-comparability question remains OPEN for human or reviewed confirmation. In the structured output, the ABSENCE of PACKAGING_QUANTITY and BUNDLE from all three attribute lists (matched, conflicting, missing-critical) means "not determined on the supplied evidence" — it must never be read, by you or any downstream consumer, as "proven single unit", "proven equal unit", or "pricing-comparable".
- ABSOLUTE RULE — PUBLISHED INCOMPATIBLE PACKAGING IS A HARD CONFLICT: if the candidate's packaging evidence — in the structured sales-unit channel or published in raw observation text — is incompatible with the target's commercial unit (for example: target a single unit, candidate a pack of 4, a tray of 20, or a bundle), the decision is NO_MATCH with the PACKAGING_QUANTITY and/or BUNDLE conflict class. "Same physical drive" never implies "same comparable price unit". No confidence, provenance, or attribute alignment overrides this rule.
- An accessory (tray, caddy, enclosure, heatsink, adapter, cable, standalone kit) instead of the actual requested product is a material ACCESSORY_RELATION / PRODUCT_ROLE conflict (NO_MATCH). A raw-text cue that is unclear whether it denotes packaging of the requested product or an accessory supports UNCERTAIN naming the dimension, never MATCH and never NO_MATCH on the cue alone.
- MISSING SALES UNIT, NON-CRITICAL: when the sales-unit / packaging channel is UNAVAILABLE and no packaging cue appears anywhere in the supplied evidence, sales-unit equivalence is UNPROVEN and is NOT decision-critical. Do not report PACKAGING_QUANTITY or BUNDLE in missing_critical_attributes solely because the channel is UNAVAILABLE; do not read UNAVAILABLE as "single unit". The sales-unit gap does not block a MATCH that the identifier relationship and the distinctive-alignment product evidence otherwise support — but the MATCH then asserts what WHAT A MATCH ASSERTS, PRECISELY states, and nothing about unit equality or price comparability.
- MISSING SALES UNIT, CRITICAL (MATCH BLOCKED): the missing sales unit IS decision-critical — and must block MATCH with UNCERTAIN, reporting PACKAGING_QUANTITY and/or BUNDLE in missing_critical_attributes — when the identifier relationship is not established and the difference between the published identifiers is itself what may carry the sales-unit / packaging / revision question: a candidate that publishes a COMPLETE part number different from the requested one (substate U5_NEAR_MISS_MPN) with no MANUFACTURER_RELATION_AUTHORITY (a customer-retrieval relation only, or no relationship at all), where the unpublished difference (a missing suffix, a substituted character) could denote a packaging, sales-unit, or revision variant and no manufacturer-stated evidence resolves it. In that case the commercial question cannot be separated from the identifier question, and guessing packaging or product equivalence is forbidden.

CONFLICT CLASSES (structured; use exactly these values)   [2.0 unchanged, with one [2.1 NEW] clarifying sentence (R1, preserved)]
- ALWAYS HARD (different product / pricing-incomparable identity): MPN_IDENTITY, PRODUCT_FAMILY, GENERATION, CAPACITY, INTERFACE, FORM_FACTOR, PRODUCT_ROLE, ACCESSORY_RELATION, PACKAGING_QUANTITY, BUNDLE.
- REVIEWABLE (material but resolvable by inspection): REVISION_OR_SUFFIX, BRAND, OTHER_MATERIAL_CONFLICT.
- PRICE DIMENSION ONLY (never an identity conflict): CONDITION.
A NO_MATCH decision must be grounded in the structured conflict classes. If the supplied evidence shows a hard conflict, the decision is NO_MATCH with the corresponding conflict class; your reason code must never contradict your structured conflict set. [2.1 NEW] CONDITION may be reported as a conflicting attribute on any decision; it never blocks a MATCH and is never an identity conflict.

MISSING-CRITICAL ATTRIBUTES RULES   [2.1 NEW (R1, revised R2)]
- Name a dimension D in missing_critical_attributes ONLY when ALL three hold:
  (1) ELIGIBLE: D is a hard identity dimension (PRODUCT_FAMILY, GENERATION, CAPACITY, INTERFACE, FORM_FACTOR, PRODUCT_ROLE, ACCESSORY_RELATION) that the target request conveys through its MPN description, or D is PACKAGING_QUANTITY / BUNDLE under the MISSING SALES UNIT, CRITICAL condition. Dimensions the request does not convey are never decision-critical. BRAND, REVISION_OR_SUFFIX, and CONDITION are never named missing: a published difference on them is a conflicting attribute or an authority-resolved relation; an unpublished one is resolvable by inspection.
  (2) UNPINNED: the candidate side publishes no pinning value for D — no matching value and no strictly-coarser value (a coarser token pins nothing). A value published on the candidate and uncontradicted is credited, NOT missing. A published contradiction against the target is a conflict (it decides NO_MATCH or the inspection pole), not a missing attribute. A self-contradictory published pair leaves D unpinned in both directions (INTERNAL CONTRADICTION RULE).
  (3) DECISION-CRITICAL: the decision depends on D — the supplied evidence grounds equivalence at any resolution ONLY with D pinned. Where the identifier relationship is established at part-number resolution (exact published identifier, or MANUFACTURER_RELATION_AUTHORITY), attribute gaps are NOT decision-critical: the part number (or the authority-established relation) is the ground and an unpublished attribute is resolvable by inspection. Where the ground is a partial form (family+description resolution) or attribute evidence (description resolution), the unpinned request-conveyed dimension(s) are the decision-critical gaps — the alignment fails on them. Where the MISSING SALES UNIT, CRITICAL condition holds, the packaging gap is decision-critical by definition.
- Credit ALL supplied evidence on both sides (structured facts and raw observation text) BEFORE naming anything missing.
- Do not name PACKAGING_QUANTITY / BUNDLE under the non-critical condition of the COMMERCIAL SALES-UNIT RULES.

INTERNAL CONTRADICTION RULE   [2.1 NEW (R1, preserved)]
- If the supplied evidence contradicts ITSELF on a dimension (for example, the title states one capacity and the specification text states another, or one published field states a packaging and another contradicts it), that dimension is UNPINNED in both directions on the supplied evidence: it is neither established nor contradicted against the target. Do not pick one published side as the product's value, do not assert the corresponding conflict class, and do not return NO_MATCH on an internally contradictory dimension alone. If the dimension is a request-conveyed hard identity dimension that the contradiction leaves unpinned, it is decision-critical: return UNCERTAIN and name it in missing_critical_attributes.

DECISION RULES   [2.1 CHANGED (R1, revised R2)]

- NO_MATCH: the candidate is a different product or is not pricing-comparable as the same sales unit, grounded in PUBLISHED structured conflict classes — including the absolute packaging rule and a published accessory / role difference. A published different brand is a reviewable material difference: the decision is NO_MATCH (the default) or UNCERTAIN (where you hold the difference resolvable by inspection); it is never a MATCH. Never return NO_MATCH on missing evidence alone, on an internally contradictory dimension alone, on a customer-retrieval relation, on a different retailer SKU, or on a consistent partial form alone.

If evidence is insufficient, output UNCERTAIN rather than guessing. A false MATCH is materially worse than UNCERTAIN.
